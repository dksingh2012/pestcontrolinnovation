Pest Prevention Guide for Gurugram Homes & Businesses

Simple, practical steps to make your property less attractive to common pests. Use this checklist alongside professional inspection when an infestation is established.

Keep food and waste under control

  • Store dry food, pet food and ingredients in sealed containers.
  • Clean food spills, grease and crumbs promptly, especially around kitchens and appliances.
  • Use covered waste bins and remove household or business waste regularly.
  • Keep food-storage and preparation areas clean and dry.

Remove standing water and excess moisture

  • Empty water collected in trays, buckets, coolers and unused containers.
  • Check plant-pot trays, roof drains, balconies and other places where water can collect.
  • Repair leaking taps and pipes; dry damp areas where possible.
  • Keep water tanks properly covered and arrange regular checks of drains.

Close common pest entry points

  • Fit or repair door sweeps, window screens and drain covers where suitable.
  • Seal gaps around pipes, cables, doors and wall openings with appropriate materials.
  • Keep storage areas organised and avoid clutter against walls.
  • Inspect incoming cartons, furniture and deliveries for signs of pests.

Check bedrooms and furniture for bedbugs

  • Inspect mattress seams, bed frames and nearby furniture when travelling or after bringing in used furniture.
  • Keep luggage off beds where practical when travelling.
  • Do not move potentially infested furniture into other rooms before getting advice.
  • Wash and dry suitable fabrics according to their care labels.

Reduce termite risk

  • Fix plumbing leaks and avoid persistent dampness near wooden fittings.
  • Keep firewood, cardboard and timber away from direct contact with building walls and soil where possible.
  • Look for mud tubes, damaged wood or unexplained hollow-sounding timber.
  • Arrange an inspection if you suspect termite activity.

Protect shops, offices and warehouses

  • Keep loading bays, pantry areas and waste zones clean and uncluttered.
  • Store goods off the floor where practical and maintain inspection access around stock.
  • Check deliveries and packaging for pest activity before storage.
  • Record sightings, locations and dates so recurring hotspots can be identified.

When should you request professional pest control?

Consider a professional inspection if you repeatedly see pests, find droppings or damage, notice termite mud tubes, suspect bedbugs, or cannot identify the source of recurring activity. Treatment should be selected for the pest, site conditions and occupants. Follow the service provider's preparation and re-entry instructions, and keep children and pets away from treated areas as directed.
