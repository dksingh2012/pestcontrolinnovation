Cockroach control for kitchens, homes and commercial sites

Cockroaches are often noticed in kitchens, cupboards, drains, storage areas and places where food residue or moisture is available. Sightings during the day, droppings, egg cases or repeated activity in the same locations can indicate that the problem needs attention.

Control depends on identifying the affected areas, access to hiding places, sanitation conditions and the type of property. A kitchen in a home, a restaurant, an office pantry and a food-storage area may require different preparation and treatment planning.

Before the visit, clean up food crumbs, store food in closed containers and note where cockroaches are seen most often. Do not apply multiple household products over the same area before a professional assessment, as this can complicate treatment planning.

Frequently asked questions

Why do cockroaches keep appearing in kitchens?

Food residue, moisture, warmth and small hiding places can support cockroach activity. The source and access points should be assessed.
