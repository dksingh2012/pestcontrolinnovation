Rat and mouse control: inspection and prevention

Scratching sounds, droppings, gnaw marks, damaged food packaging and repeated sightings can indicate rodent activity. The source may be inside a building, around waste-storage areas or near gaps that allow rodents to enter.

Effective rodent management starts by identifying activity and possible entry points. Depending on the site, a plan may include monitoring, suitable control measures, advice on proofing gaps and improvements to food and waste storage. The right approach differs between a home, restaurant, office, warehouse and other commercial property.

When you enquire, tell PCI where signs have appeared, how often rodents are seen and whether food, electrical wiring or stored goods may be affected. Keep food sealed and waste contained while arranging an assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What are common signs of rats or mice?

Droppings, gnaw marks, damaged packaging, scratching noises and repeated sightings are common warning signs.

Can rodent entry points be identified?

A site assessment can help locate likely access points and conditions that may be attracting rodents.

What details help you estimate rodent-control requirements?

Share your city, property type, affected areas, frequency of sightings and any known entry points.
