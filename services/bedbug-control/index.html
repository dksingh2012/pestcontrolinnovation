Bedbug treatment: signs, inspection and follow-up

Bedbugs may leave itchy bites, small dark spotting on bedding, shed skins or live insects around mattress seams, bed frames and nearby furniture. Bites alone do not confirm bedbugs, so inspection of the sleeping area and surrounding furniture is important.

Treatment planning depends on the extent of activity, the rooms and furniture affected, property occupancy and preparation requirements. Homes, hotels, hostels and other shared accommodation may need coordinated inspection and follow-up so that activity is not missed in adjoining areas.

When requesting an assessment, tell PCI which rooms are affected, when activity was first noticed and whether furniture or luggage has recently been moved. Avoid moving potentially affected bedding or furniture into other rooms before receiving advice.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check for bedbugs?

Look for live insects, small dark spotting, shed skins or eggs around mattress seams, bed frames and nearby furniture. An inspection is needed to confirm.

Do bedbug problems need follow-up?

Follow-up needs depend on the infestation, property and treatment method. Ask for the recommended monitoring and next steps for your site.

What should I do before a bedbug-treatment visit?

Describe the affected rooms and follow the preparation instructions provided by the service team. Avoid moving suspected infested items to other rooms.
