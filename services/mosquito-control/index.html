Mosquito control: identify breeding risks and activity

Mosquito activity can be influenced by standing water in containers, drains, roof areas, plant trays and other places where water collects. The first step is to understand where mosquitoes are being noticed and whether potential breeding sites are present.

A site-specific mosquito-management plan may consider source reduction, inspection of the surroundings and appropriate targeted measures. The approach for an apartment complex, garden, office campus or commercial property can differ, so the property and service requirement should be discussed before work is scheduled.

Before contacting PCI, note the areas where mosquitoes are most active, the time of day when the problem is worst and any visible water-collection points. Removing avoidable standing water and keeping containers covered can support prevention.

Frequently asked questions

Does mosquito control include checking standing water?

Identifying water-collection points is an important part of understanding mosquito activity. The exact inspection and service scope should be confirmed for your property.

Is the same mosquito-control plan suitable for every property?

No. Surroundings, water sources, property size and the type of site affect the recommended approach.

What should I tell PCI before a mosquito-control visit?

Share your city, property type, affected outdoor or indoor areas, and any standing-water locations you have noticed.
