Termite control: signs, inspection and treatment planning

Termite activity may be noticed through damaged or hollow-sounding wood, mud tubes, discarded wings or recurring damage around wooden fittings. These signs can have other causes, so an on-site assessment is the sensible first step before choosing a treatment.

A suitable termite-management plan depends on where activity is found, the construction type, access to affected areas and whether the issue is in a building, furniture or stored material. PCI can discuss the property and recommend the next step after reviewing the problem.

Before the visit, note the affected rooms, when you first noticed the signs and whether the activity appears in more than one location. Avoid disturbing suspected mud tubes or damaged areas until they can be inspected.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my property has termites?

Possible signs include mud tubes, damaged or hollow-sounding wood, discarded wings and recurring timber damage. An inspection is needed to confirm the cause.

Can termite treatment be planned for an occupied home?

The suitable method and any preparation depend on the property and treatment selected. Ask PCI for site-specific instructions before work begins.

What should I share when requesting termite control?

Share your city, property type, affected rooms or wooden items, and any visible signs. Photos can help explain the issue.
